Hutton Cipher: What Has—and Hasn't—Been Verified

October 27, 2026 · Research status

Short version: MysteryTwister C3 Part 5 is the target. An official FEDORA/JUPITER test vector has been reproduced. No verified Part 5 key or plaintext is documented.

What is established

  • The research target is the Hutton cipher challenge identified as MysteryTwister Part 5.
  • An official FEDORA/JUPITER test vector was reproduced in the work so far.
  • That reproduction checks an implementation; it does not establish a solution to Part 5.

What is not established

No candidate Part 5 key or plaintext has been independently verified, and no solve is claimed here.

Proposed acceptance test

A candidate should decrypt the published challenge, then re-encrypt the resulting plaintext with the candidate key and reproduce the exact published ciphertext. Until that check passes and the candidate can be reproduced, it remains a hypothesis—not a result.

Next

A useful update requires a reproducible candidate, exact inputs and outputs, and the re-encryption check. Until then, the status stays unsolved.
