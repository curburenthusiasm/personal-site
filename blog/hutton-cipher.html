Hutton Cipher: What Has—and Hasn't—Been Verified

October 27, 2026 · Robert Foley · Research status

Short version: MysteryTwister C3 Part 5 is the target. I have reproduced an official FEDORA/JUPITER test vector. I do not have a verified Part 5 key or plaintext to report.

What is established

  • The research target is the Hutton cipher challenge identified as MysteryTwister Part 5.
  • An official FEDORA/JUPITER test vector was reproduced in the work so far.
  • That test-vector reproduction is an implementation check. It is not evidence that the Part 5 challenge has been solved.

What is not established

No candidate Part 5 key or plaintext has been independently verified, and no solve is claimed here. There are no challenge results to report yet.

Proposed acceptance test

A candidate should decrypt the published challenge and then re-encrypt the resulting plaintext with the candidate key to reproduce the exact published ciphertext. Until that exact-match check passes and the candidate can be reproduced, it remains a hypothesis—not a result.

Next

The next useful update is evidence: a reproducible candidate, the exact inputs and outputs, and the re-encryption check. Until then, the status stays unsolved.
