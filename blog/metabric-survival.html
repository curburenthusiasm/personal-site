METABRIC Survival Modeling: Protocol Before Results

October 27, 2026 · Robert Foley · Research status

Status: The experimental runner and evaluation protocol exist. A completed formal run with reportable metrics was not available for this update. No model-performance result is claimed.

Targets, not results

The planned evaluation uses ten repetitions of five-fold validation. The protocol's stated targets are:

  • Mean integrated Brier score (IBS) at or below 0.167.
  • Mean time-dependent concordance at or above 0.665.
  • An IBS gain greater than twice the repeat-to-repeat standard deviation.

These are acceptance targets. No completed formal-run means, variability estimates, or target attainment are available to report here.

What a result update needs

A useful report will identify the evaluated comparison, summarize the ten-by-five-fold runs, show the mean and repeat-to-repeat variation, and say whether each target was met. Until those runs are complete and reviewable, the metrics remain pending.

Scope

This is an exploratory research project using breast-cancer survival data. It is not a clinical tool, is not validated for patient care, and should not be used to guide diagnosis or treatment.
