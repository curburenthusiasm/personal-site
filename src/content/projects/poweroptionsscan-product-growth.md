## Problem

Building a digital product is only half the problem; distribution, conversion, and learning from user behavior determine whether the product can become a business.

## Approach

I built PowerOptionsScan as an options-scanning product and instrumented the path from registration through dashboard, upgrade, and checkout. The surrounding growth stack included analytics, session behavior, email capture and drip campaigns, SEO content, and experiments across paid and organic distribution channels.

## What’s interesting

- Funnel stages are measured explicitly rather than inferred from traffic.
- Product development and distribution experiments are treated as one feedback loop.
- Failed channels are documented as evidence about friction rather than hidden as unsuccessful attempts.

## Results / Status

The product was launched and marketed, producing real traffic and checkout activity. The project remains useful as a case study in product building, instrumentation, and the difficulty of distribution.
