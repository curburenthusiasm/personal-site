## Problem

A consulting offer is only useful if qualified prospects can be found consistently. Generic lead lists create noise; EDI consulting needs companies with credible integration pain, trading-partner complexity, or modernization pressure.

## Approach

I built an agent-assisted workflow for FirstPassEDI that researches companies, looks for observable EDI pain signals, gathers supporting evidence, scores prospects, identifies likely contacts, and drafts outreach based on the specific problem found.

## What’s interesting

- Prospecting starts from evidence of operational pain rather than demographic list buying.
- Research, scoring, and outreach preparation are separate stages so weak evidence can be rejected early.
- The workflow is designed to create a repeatable pipeline for a niche technical service without relying on paid bidding channels.

## Results / Status

The workflow is in active use and has produced qualified leads for the consulting business.
